# Product

## Register

product

## Users

PC rhythm-game players using an Android phone as a low-latency, multi-touch controller. They configure the tool briefly, then need it to disappear into play without obscuring the game or trapping them in a mode.

## Product Purpose

Turn a phone into an accessible, configurable rhythm-game input surface while mirroring hand position on the PC. Success means dependable input, clear setup, low latency, and immediate recovery from configuration or connection mistakes.

## Brand Personality

Focused, responsive, and approachable. The interface should feel like a trustworthy utility made by someone who understands rhythm-game flow.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative gaming overlays, dense control panels, hidden mode changes, tiny precision-only targets, and visual effects that compete with gameplay.

## Design Principles

- Keep play unobstructed while preserving an obvious route back to editing.
- Make every mode and connection state legible at a glance.
- Prefer forgiving targets and familiar desktop interactions.
- Keep visual work off the latency-critical input path.
- Preserve user configuration and recover safely from disconnects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, do not rely on color alone for state, honor reduced-motion system settings, provide keyboard and pointer paths for core actions, and avoid precision-only interactions.
