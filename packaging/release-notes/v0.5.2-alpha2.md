# Doritrack v0.5.2-alpha2 — Faster reconnects, less Wi-Fi traffic

Prerelease on top of v0.5.2-alpha1. Install the Windows and Android apps from
this same release: both sides changed.

## Faster recovery after a dropout

- **The PC tells the phone right away.** When the PC controller stops or its
  link fails, it now sends the phone an authenticated end-of-session message.
  The phone used to wait up to 64 ms (finger down) or 2 seconds (idle) to
  notice on its own.
- **No lost taps right after a reconnect.** The phone used to wait one round
  trip after the PC said it was ready before sending input again, and a quick
  tap in that window disappeared. It now starts sending at once; the PC still
  applies everything in order after the clean session boundary.
- **Reconnects go straight to the PC.** The phone first contacts the PC it was
  just connected to, then broadcasts as before. On Wi-Fi, broadcasts are sent
  at the slowest rate with no retry and can be held back by a sleeping PC
  adapter. Retries now start after 4 ms instead of 100 ms.
- **No key-store unlock per reconnect.** The phone unlocks its pairing key
  once per Start instead of twice on every reconnect, one of which briefly
  blocked the touch screen thread.

## Less network traffic during play

- The PC no longer acknowledges the phone's immediate backup copy of each
  touch sample, cutting the PC's replies from three to two per sample. Real
  repairs of lost packets are still acknowledged.
- While waiting for the phone, the PC repeats its ready message less often.
- The phone's network socket is now locked to the paired PC.
- On Linux, the PC's replies are marked for the Wi-Fi voice queue (DSCP EF),
  matching the phone. Windows needs an administrator QoS policy for this, so
  it is not requested there.

## Downloads

- Windows x64: `Doritrack-v0.5.2-alpha2-windows-x64.zip`.
- Android: `Doritrack-v0.5.2-alpha2-android.apk`, version code 40. It updates
  earlier v0.5.x builds in place, keeping settings and pairing.

Automated loopback testing kept healthy delivery and recovery after loss of
both immediate copies within the 8.333 ms design budget (worst observed
3.2 ms). These tests simulate OS acceptance. The faster reconnect path has not
yet been soaked on real phones, cables and routers; please report any
reconnect that takes longer or behaves differently than before.
