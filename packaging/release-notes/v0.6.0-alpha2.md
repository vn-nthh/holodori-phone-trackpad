# Doritrack v0.6.0-alpha2 — USB tether route protection fix

Prerelease on top of v0.6.0-alpha1 (experimental protocol V6). Install the
Windows and Android apps from this same release. The Android app is unchanged
apart from its version number.

## USB tether route protection (Windows)

With **Don't use the phone's internet** on, Start could fail with:

> could not protect the USB tether route ... the tether adapter disconnected
> during recovery; reconnect it before enabling local-only tethering

even though the phone was connected. This happened when an earlier session
had protected the adapter's IPv6 settings and IPv6 was later turned off on
that adapter. The PC tried to finish that old IPv6 recovery first, could not
without IPv6, and then refused to protect the IPv4 route as well.

The PC now skips an address family the adapter does not currently have. There
is nothing to protect there, and the old recovery record is kept until that
family comes back. If recovery still cannot finish while the family is
present, Start is refused as before, with a clearer message.

## Update both devices together

V6 is not wire-compatible with V5. A v0.6 PC host will not talk to a v0.5.x
phone app, and the reverse. Pairing identities are unchanged, and the Android
app updates v0.5.x and v0.6.0-alpha1 in place, keeping settings and pairing.
