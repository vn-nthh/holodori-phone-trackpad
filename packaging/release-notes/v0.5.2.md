# Doritrack v0.5.2

Stable release of the v0.5.2 alpha series: faster recovery after a dropout,
less network traffic during play, optional same-lane tap spacing, and safer
USB route handling. Install the Windows and Android apps from this same
release: both sides changed.

## Faster recovery after a dropout

- **The PC tells the phone right away** when the controller stops or its link
  fails, with an authenticated end-of-session message. The phone used to wait
  up to 64 ms (finger down) or 2 seconds (idle) to notice on its own.
- **No lost taps right after a reconnect.** The phone starts sending as soon as
  the PC says it is ready, instead of waiting one more round trip. The PC still
  applies everything in order after the clean session boundary.
- **Reconnects go straight to the PC** the phone was just connected to, then
  broadcast as before. Retries start after 4 ms instead of 100 ms.
- **No key-store unlock per reconnect.** The phone unlocks its pairing key once
  per Start, not twice on every reconnect.

## Less network traffic during play

- The PC no longer acknowledges the phone's immediate backup copy of each touch
  sample. Real repairs of lost packets are still acknowledged.
- While waiting for the phone, the PC repeats its ready message less often.
- The phone's network socket is locked to the paired PC.
- The phone marks its traffic for the Wi-Fi voice queue (DSCP EF). On Linux the
  PC marks its replies too; Windows needs an administrator QoS policy for that.

## Separate fast same-lane taps (beta, off by default)

Turn on **Settings > Separate fast same-lane taps (beta)** in the PC app if a
fast re-tap on one lane is sometimes missed. Same-lane key changes that would
reach Windows at the same instant are spaced by a fixed 2 ms:

- a re-press right after a release waits 2 ms;
- a finger landing on a lane another finger holds sends a release, then a press
  2 ms later;
- a tap delivered in one burst after a network repair is held for 2 ms.

Slides and taps on different lanes are never delayed. A tap is delayed by at
most about 6 ms.

## Reliability

- **Windows timer precision.** The PC host requests a 1 ms timer period while it
  runs, so its stuck-key watchdog and recovery timing are not stretched to
  Windows' default 15.6 ms tick.
- **Stuck-key safety net.** If the PC controller is killed or crashes, the PC
  app releases all six lane keys.
- **USB tether route recovery (Windows).** With **Don't use the phone's
  internet** on, the PC could fail to restore the phone's internet route after
  play and leave its recovery record behind. It now confirms the gateway through
  the adapter's own configuration, skips routes from older tether sessions, and
  completes recovery on phones that change their USB network address every
  session (such as Samsung).

## Downloads and upgrade

- Windows x64: extract `Doritrack-v0.5.2-windows-x64.zip` and run
  `HolodoriUsbController.exe`.
- Android: install `Doritrack-v0.5.2-android.apk`, version code 42. It updates
  v0.5.1 and the v0.5.2 alphas in place, keeping settings and pairing.

Some antivirus products, including Microsoft Defender's machine-learning
detection, may flag the unsigned controller executable. Each download's SHA-256
is listed on this release page and in the zip's `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Automated loopback testing kept healthy delivery and recovery after loss of
both immediate copies within the 8.333 ms design budget. These tests simulate
OS acceptance and do not establish latency on every phone, cable, router, PC,
or game input loop.

terms and competitive rules.
